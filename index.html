Industrial Divisions

Specialized Technical Training Divisions

We organize all 229 technical and occupational safety modules into four specialized corporate divisions for easy HSE procurement and worker compliance.

Division 01

Lifting & Rigging Operations

Certified qualifications for Mobile, Tower, Crawler, Hiab & Overhead Cranes, Rigging & Slinging, Banksmen, and Lifting Supervisors.

  • Rigger & Safe Slinging
  • Crane Operator & Supervisor
  • Banksman, Hoists & Winches
Division 02

Scaffolding & Height Safety

Specialized training for Working at Height, Scaffolding Erection & Dismantling, Scafftag Inspectors, Rope Access, and BMU/TSP Cradles.

  • Work at Height & Fall Protection
  • Scaffolding Erector & Inspector
  • Rope Access, Cradles & Ladders
Division 03

Heavy Equipment & Machinery

Operator safety certifications for Forklifts, MEWPs, Excavators, Loaders, Dozers, Concrete Pumps, Boilers, and Workshop Machinery.

  • Forklift, Reach Truck & Telehandler
  • Scissor Lift, Boom Lift & MEWPs
  • Earthmoving & Industrial Machines
Division 04

Occupational & Technical Safety

Fire Fighting, First Aid & BLS, Confined Space & H2S, Gas Testing, COSHH/HAZMAT, Electrical/LOTO, Welding, and HSE Risk Management.

  • Fire Fighting, First Aid & Rescue
  • Confined Space, H2S & Gas Tester
  • LOTO, PTW, COSHH & Risk Assessment
Institution Profile

PROLIFT Technical Training Centre L.L.C

PROLIFT TECHNICAL TRAINING CENTRE L.L.C. is a specialized training institution dedicated to enhancing operational safety and technical skills across major industrial, logistics, and construction market segments in the United Arab Emirates.

Operating under DED Commercial License No. 1636409, KHDA Educational Services Permit No. 634197, and HFZA Registration No. 333618, we ensure our trainees achieve the highest standards of safety and operational competence required by regulatory bodies and main contractors.

COMPETENCE

Rigorous, practical training to develop highly skilled professionals.

COMPLIANCE

Ensuring all training meets local regulations & standards.

CERTIFICATION

Providing officially permitted qualifications.

Core Operational Advantages

Zero compromise on workplace safety. Our structured courses eliminate accident hazards, protect high-value machinery assets, and guarantee regulatory compliance.
